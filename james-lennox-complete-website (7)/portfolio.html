2023–24 bowling season

I recorded 13 wickets for Penrith Cricket Club during the 2023–24 season, with best bowling figures of 5/41. This is a concrete milestone in my playing development.

Reflection: The experience highlights the value of consistent preparation, focus and learning from each performance.

Junior skills session — proposed

This is a future coaching concept rather than a session I have delivered. The 60-minute plan includes a 10-minute warm-up, 15 minutes of catching and throwing, 15 minutes of batting or bowling technique, 15 minutes of small-sided games and a five-minute reflection. Activities would be adapted for age, ability and safety.

Goal: Help players enjoy practice while receiving constructive, specific feedback.

Positive team culture — proposed

This proposed coaching framework uses three habits: setting shared expectations, recognising effort and inviting player reflection. It would encourage respect, inclusion and learning from mistakes.

Goal: Support players to become confident teammates as well as capable cricketers.
