THE PERSON BEHIND THE PLAYER

ABOUT ME

From the crease to the coaching pathway.

MY STORY

Learning through cricket

I'm James Lennox, a cricketer from Penrith, Sydney, and a first-year Diploma of Social Science student.

I play for Penrith Cricket Club and am passionate about the skills and character the game develops. Cricket challenges players to stay focused, work as a team and respond positively when things do not go to plan.

My long-term ambition is to become a cricket coach who supports young and current players as individuals, not simply as statistics.

My studies in social science complement my interest in people, communication and development. I want to keep learning how to bring out the best in others.

WHAT MATTERS

My developing coaching philosophy

People first

Recognise that every player learns differently and deserves encouragement and respect.

Progress over perfection

Celebrate effort, feedback and small improvements, not only match-day results.

Team culture

Build an environment where players feel included, accountable and motivated.

These principles describe my coaching aspirations; they are not claims of formal coaching experience.
